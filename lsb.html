Saudi Business Dictionary

The terms you'll hear — explained plainly.

MISA

Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia

+

The government body that issues the foreign investment license required before a non-Saudi can register a company in the Kingdom.

CR

Commercial Registration

+

The certificate from the Ministry of Commerce that formally registers your company and confirms its legal existence in Saudi Arabia.

ZATCA

Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority

+

The authority responsible for tax registration, VAT, corporate tax, and customs compliance for businesses operating in the Kingdom.

Qiwa

Labor & Workforce Platform

+

The Ministry of Human Resources platform used to manage employment contracts, work permits, and labor compliance.

GOSI

General Organization for Social Insurance

+

The social insurance system employers register with to cover employee benefits, pensions, and occupational hazard contributions.

Muqeem

Residency & Sponsorship Platform

+

The platform used to manage residency permits (Iqama) and sponsorship status for foreign employees and residents.
